## Metric fields

- `pnl`: final cash + inventory × final reference − initial cash, after fees.
- `max_drawdown`: maximum step-end running-peak equity minus equity, including time zero.
- `avg_abs_inventory`: mean of absolute inventory at times 1…T; `max_abs_inventory`: maximum including time zero.
- `fills`, `volume`: both count unit executions, not round trips.
- `fees`: total filled notional × fee rate.
- `terminal_inventory`: remaining signed units, not silently closed.
- `arrivals`: potential customers whether or not the dealer could/should fill.
- `cap_blocked`: arriving-side opportunities disabled by inventory cap, regardless of latent acceptance.
- `paused_arrivals`: arriving side intentionally paused, after checking cap.
- `fill_rate`: fills/arrivals, zero if no arrivals.
- `spread_capture`: signed execution edge relative to reference at trade time; part of P&L, not an extra payment.
- `holding_pnl`: post-fill inventory × subsequent reference change, summed across steps.
- `accounting_error`: maximum of terminal cash reconstruction and P&L-decomposition residuals.

`pnl_ci_low/high` in summary describe uncertainty in a marginal mean. `pnl_p05/p95` describe individual-path dispersion. For policy comparisons prefer the *paired* intervals in `paired_comparisons.csv`. All intervals are unadjusted for multiple comparisons. Paths are independent across seeds within a scenario; the same seeds are reused across scenarios, so do not pool all scenario rows as independent evidence.

## Sensitivity reconstruction

Start from `base_config` in config.json. For each `setting`, modify only the named Config field: `fee_bps=0/2`, `arrival_rate=0.4/1.6`, `inventory_limit=5/20`, or `vol_multiplier=0.5/2`. `reference` uses the base config. Each setting uses seeds 20000…20029 in Stable and Adverse selection. Strategy parameters stay fixed. Thirty repeats per setting are exploratory and less precise than the main 100.

## Follow-up mechanism study

[MECHANISM_REPORT.md](../MECHANISM_REPORT.md) and
[RESEARCH_ADDENDUM_PLAN.md](../RESEARCH_ADDENDUM_PLAN.md) describe the new design.
Total new executions: 2,000 strategy runs (1,500 factorial-policy runs plus 500
independent-flow runs). The informed 500 rows are reused in the 1,000-row control
CSV and are not new runs a second time. Seeds 30000–30099 are disjoint from the
original study.

| Artifact | Purpose |
|---|---|
| [manifest](mechanisms/manifest.json) | Config, all seeds, source/plan/result hashes, counts and timing |
| [ablation runs](mechanisms/ablation_runs.csv) | Three regimes × 100 seeds × five policies |
| [ablation summary](mechanisms/ablation_summary.csv) | Means, SD, quantiles and mean intervals |
| [ablation contrasts](mechanisms/ablation_pairs.csv) | Pairwise effects and factorial interaction |
| [flow runs](mechanisms/flow_control_runs.csv) | Informed/independent counterfactuals on identical prices |
| [flow summary](mechanisms/flow_control_summary.csv) | Results by flow condition and policy |
| [flow contrasts](mechanisms/flow_control_pairs.csv) | Informed minus independent, paired by seed |
| [diagnostics](mechanisms/tape_diagnostics.csv) | Exact shared-tape checks, price hashes, direction correlations |
| [audit](mechanisms/validation.json) | Independent recomputation of 116 follow-up intervals |
| [original regression](mechanisms/original_regression.json) | Original CSV metrics versus initial public release |
| [ablation plot](figures/ablation_effects.png) | Five contrasts, three scenarios, unadjusted 95% intervals |
| [flow plot](figures/flow_control.png) | Marginal means and paired information-link effects |

Control CSV keys are scenario, seed, strategy **and flow**. Filter a flow
condition before policy contrasts; do not pool the reused informed rows as
independent evidence. Negative P&L control differences mean informative flow
was harmful inside this specific simulator.
